ii · why this
why a picturebook?
because nobody else dares.
Every SG hotpot brand fights with the same red+gold dragons. We hand-draw our own world.
1
自创IP
Owned Universe
A chicken mascot (Brother 八), a recurring grandma, a curious kid. Characters that become assets — stickers, plushies, kids menus, AR filters. Every video is a new chapter.
2
家庭友好
Family-First
Joanna's positioning: women, children, family gatherings. Illustration beats food porn for that audience — kids can read it, mums can share it, grandma understands it.
3
省成本
Repeatable IP
One illustration pack = thousands of variations. No need to re-shoot food every campaign. Comic strips for IG carousels. Stickers for WhatsApp. Animations for TikTok. One asset, many homes.
Every other SG hotpot brand Red lanterns. Gold dragons. Steaming food in saturated colour. Bold sans-serif text shouting "限时优惠". You blink, you've forgotten which brand.
vs.
潮八八 · Chao 88 A chicken in a tiny chef's hat ladling soup for a cartoon grandma. You smile. You remember. You tag a friend whose mum would love it.

Four broths · four illustrated portraits
Each of the 4 signature chicken broths becomes a character of its own. Same illustrated jar / pot frame, different ingredient cast inside, different colour temperament. Reusable across menus, posters, IG carousels.

黑金麻辣鸡煲
The one for the Sichuan-curious. Layered chili oil, slow-built numb-spicy. Brother Eight calls it "the loud cousin".
