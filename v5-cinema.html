WHY THIS

四个理由 · 押注映画

— four reasons to bet on the cinema —
REASON · 一

感性高于功能

Family / wellness diners are emotionally driven. A cinematic short delivers feeling per second far higher than a recipe shot. People share what makes them feel something — discount codes don't viral, films do.

REASON · 二

Higgsfield原生

AI video generation excels at chiaroscuro, slow-motion, food close-ups, steam — exactly this aesthetic. Your new Higgsfield subscription becomes a production studio. One LUT, infinite scenes.

REASON · 三

定位向上一个档

Mid-tier SGD 35-60 brands all look the same — bright, cluttered, loud. Cinema-grade content lifts the perceived value 1.5-2 tiers. Same dish, different signal. Customers pay for the story they're walking into.

REASON · 四

奖项与PR可能

Picturebook is loved. Cinema is reviewed. Films get talked about in F&B trade press, restaurant award juries, design blogs. Joanna ends up on stage at SG Restaurant Week, not behind a counter.
