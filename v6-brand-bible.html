ii · brand
Chapter 一 · The Story

深耕潮汕
风味

Rooted in Teochew tradition

潮八八深耕潮汕风味，甄选地道好料，精工细作，恪守清鲜本味，严选新鲜食材，主打温润食补养生风味。

鲜鸡鲜汤、特色风味主食，皆是潮汕非遗地道滋味，藏着深入人心的家常好味，陪伴日常烟火三餐。

坚守传统匠心烹饪，传承地道饮食风味，还原食材原生鲜香，用心做好每一味健康佳肴，让食客尽享最纯粹美味。

Chao Baba inherits classic Teochew flavors, uses fresh premium ingredients and refined cooking, focusing on mild and nourishing food. Local staples are authentic Teochew traditional tastes, bringing warm homely flavour for daily dining. We uphold traditional craftsmanship to retain original ingredient freshness, serving wholesome delicacies with genuine pure taste.

v · dipping philosophy
Chapter 四 · the sauce

蘸料哲学

Dipping Philosophy
潮八八 鸡火锅，
蘸料永远是配角。

一勺沙姜、蒜蓉，
一点生抽，
不为掩盖鸡肉的鲜，
只为把本味轻轻托起。

潮八八 坚持，
用最朴素蘸料，
还原跑山鸡最本真的鲜甜。
Chao Baba Chicken Hot Pot lets ingredients shine. A spoonful of ginger sauce and garlic, with a dash of soy sauce — we never overpower fresh chicken, only lift its natural taste. With our simple signature combo, we retain the most authentic flavor of free-range chicken.
